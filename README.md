# nextstep

nextstep is a Kanban dashboard for organizing your job and internship search. Save opportunities you’re interested in, track your applications, and see what’s coming up.

**[Open nextstep in browser](https://mynextstep.pages.dev/)**

## What you can do

- Save job postings, application deadlines, and recruiter contact details before you apply.
- Move cards across the board as your applications progress, from **Interested** to **Completed**.
- Set follow-up reminders and find applications that are due in the **Follow-up** column.
- See upcoming interviews and follow-ups in the calendar, and open the related application from any event.
- Keep notes and review your application history in each card.

## Guest mode and accounts

Choose **Continue as guest** to start without an account. Your guest board is saved in your current browser. Clearing the site’s data deletes it.

Sign in to access your account board across browsers and devices. Guest and account boards are separate.
