# nextstep

nextstep is a Kanban dashboard for organizing your job and internship search. Keep track of where you’ve applied, when to reach out again, and what happened along the way.

**[Open nextstep in browser](https://mynextstep.pages.dev/)**

## What you can do

### Organize your search

- Save a role in **Interested** with the posting link, application deadline, and recruiter details. Mark it as applied when you send your application, or use **In Contact** to track an opportunity that started with a conversation.
- Drag cards between columns or reorder them within a column. Pin important opportunities so they stay at the top of their current column.
- Search by company or role, filter by job or internship, and choose which columns to show. The dashboard gives you a quick count of total opportunities, active applications, due follow-ups, and completed applications.
- Applying for another role at the same company? Duplicate an opportunity to start a fresh card in **Interested** with the company, location, and recruiter details filled in.

### Stay on top of follow-ups

- Set a reminder for a specific date or a number of days after you applied or made contact. When it’s due, the card appears in **Follow-up**.
- Open the card to find the recruiter’s details and use the shortcuts to email, call, or open LinkedIn when those details are saved.
- After reaching out, mark the follow-up done or schedule another one. If you need more time, reschedule the existing reminder and it stays open. The card’s history records when you complete or reschedule a follow-up.

### Track interviews and outcomes

- Save the interview date, time, location or meeting link, and notes. If you’re invited to another round, schedule it and keep the previous interview details on the same card.
- Move an application to **Awaiting decision** after an interview or assessment. Add a note to remember what you’re waiting for, and see how long you’ve been waiting on the board.
- Close an application with an outcome such as **Accepted**, **Rejected**, **Withdrawn**, or **No response**. Reopen the card anytime to review your notes and application history.

### Plan ahead with the calendar

See interviews, follow-up reminders, and deadlines for roles you’re interested in. Use the dashboard calendar for a quick look at what’s coming up, or open the monthly view to browse dates. You can add events to existing applications from the calendar and open the related card from an event.

## Guest mode and accounts

Choose **Continue as guest** to start without an account. Your guest board stays in your current browser after you close it. Clearing the site’s data deletes it.

Sign in to access your account board across browsers and devices. If you have a guest board in the same browser, you can copy its applications into your account. The guest copy stays in the browser.

The interface is available in English and Swedish, with light and dark themes.
